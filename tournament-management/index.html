Available division tiles show an approximate start and total ring time on one extra line; no hover is needed. Each start assumes assigning that division next to the earliest projected available ring, independently of other available divisions. It is not a reservation or automatic assignment. Start TBD means no usable prediction; ring time can still be shown. Ring time assumes everyone competes in all applicable disciplines, including Weapons only where applicable. Seven minutes per saved competitor is a rough total baseline, not a learned average or a separate calculation for each discipline. Enter at least two characters of a first name, last name, or rank. Separate multiple terms with commas: Anna, G1 finds anyone matching Anna OR G1, not only people matching both. Spaces around terms and duplicate terms are ignored; terms shorter than two characters are ignored. Matching is case-insensitive and literal; all matching saved competitors for the active event appear once with matching text highlighted. Search updates as you type; Search or Enter also runs it. Studio, parent, and phone are not search fields. Use advanced filters for studio, division, or ring rather than typing Crescent D2 or Division 76 into the name/rank search. Select a competitor name to open complete details. Click the Competitor heading to reverse name order.

Filters opens a compact optional panel. Apply filters combines name, rank, studio, division, ring, and unassigned conditions with AND, within the comma-separated OR search results. A blank search with applied filters searches the saved roster using those filters alone; a nonempty search still needs a two-character term. Name matches the full name as a literal substring. Show only unassigned means no identifiable current, queued, or completed ring assignment; unknown ring data is not treated as unassigned. Clear filters restores the existing name/rank search. Closing Filters hides the controls but preserves applied filters. Options come from the active event. All matches are displayed without pagination; the footer reports actual counts.

Details show first name, last name, parent name, phone, rank, studio, saved group division, and identifiable ring. Current and queued assignments are distinguished from completed ring history; conflicting assignments are marked ambiguous rather than guessed. Missing registration fields say Not provided. Refreshes occur every ten seconds. Failed refreshes clear old personal details; if only ring data fails, registration remains searchable with ring assignments explicitly unavailable.
