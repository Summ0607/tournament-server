Import volunteers from CSV

Headers: First Name, Last Name, DOB, Assoc ID, School, Rank, Certification, Primary Volunteer Type, Secondary Volunteer Type. Optional columns: Parent/Guardian Name, Contact Number, Gender, Email. Guardian name is required under 18. Omitted optional columns preserve existing values. DOB: YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY, converted to age on the tournament date and not stored. Certification maps to the shared levels (Unknown, Uncertified, SK/TK, Corner, Regional (Center), International); SK/TK requires age 16+ at G5 or higher, or age 14+ at CDB; In-House tournaments use age 11+ for SK/TK and 15+ for Corner, with rank requirements unchanged. Unrecognized values are stored as Unknown. Certification and Secondary may be blank. An Association ID is required except at In-House events, where non-member IDs may be blank (add-only).

Volunteer preparation rules

Volunteers and editable types belong to the selected tournament. Operational use locks changes until Reset Run. CSV imports validate completely before saving; duplicate nonblank Assoc IDs within a file are rejected. Existing nonblank IDs update; blank IDs always add. Primary type is required; secondary is optional and must differ. Type names in CSV must match existing types (case-insensitive). SK/TK certification requires G5 or higher at age 16+, or CDB at age 14+, on the tournament date. Only volunteers with an Association ID can be added to Ring Judges; non-member In-House volunteers are never judges. The Ring Judges directory can include lower-ranked SK/TK volunteers when they are assigned the Judging type; Dan-ranked volunteers aged 18+ remain eligible as judge candidates. Population copies supplied certification and does not confirm availability. Ring Judges edits update linked volunteers with qualification validation; edits or deletion on the Volunteers page do not automatically change copied judges.

Population and phase-planning rules

Ring Judges contains judge and SK/TK candidates for the selected tournament, not necessarily the active tournament. Operational use locks edits until Reset Run. Competitor population adds Dan-ranked competitors aged 18+ as judge candidates and adds competitors meeting SK/TK rank and age rules (G5 or higher at age 16+, CDB at age 14+). Their certification remains Unknown, and SK/TK eligibility is noted for verification. Volunteer population adds primary/secondary Judging volunteers who are Dan-ranked and 18+, or who have SK/TK certification and meet its rank and age requirements; supplied certification is copied. Existing linked identities and Association IDs are not duplicated; existing entries are not overwritten. Certification and availability must be verified. Priority is an editable value from 0 to 99; use its column filter to narrow the directory. Use P1/P2 Judge Assignments to plan phase staffing; Manual Only remains unavailable.
