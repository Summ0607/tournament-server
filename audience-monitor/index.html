Approximate start and finish times, not call times. Subject to change. Please follow staff announcements.

In the Ring shows compact rows in fixed ring order: ring, active division and description, a thin overall-progress bar, and estimated finish. Overall progress is approximate and weighted by modeled phase duration; individual phases and percentages are not displayed. Missing progress or estimates are labeled unavailable. Paused and disconnected rings show a neutral message rather than old progress or finish times. Queued divisions appear only in On Deck, not as active divisions. This page trials the saved Visual Definitions colors. Refresh the page after saving color changes; font sizes and layout remain audience-specific.

Only assigned queued divisions appear. The next division for every ring takes priority over later queue entries; within each queue position, divisions are ordered by estimated start and ring. Each compact row shows ring, division, description, and estimated start. Missing estimates say Not available. Predictions are low-confidence planning estimates, not guaranteed call times. Unassigned hypothetical suggestions are not displayed. Follow staff announcements.

Compact rows and additional columns on wide screens keep more rings visible. Sections stay stationary when all rows fit; overflow pages rotate independently every 12 seconds. Nothing scrolls continuously. Data refreshes every 10 seconds. Ring order stays fixed; queue updates retain existing order within each queue position until the next rotation. Display controls contains source selection, Pause pages, and Fullscreen. Pause pages stops rotation, not refresh. Stale or failed data hides predictions and progress; the display recovers automatically. Unattended read-only access lasts 12 hours from sign-in, without extending access to editable workspaces. Sign-out, account disabling, and session revocation still take effect.
